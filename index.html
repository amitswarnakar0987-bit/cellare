Beyond storage

We create spaces worthy of what you collect.

A cellar is not a room for bottles. It is a composition of timber, stone, climate and light — an architecture of patience that holds your collection in the dark, at the right degree, for decades.

From the first drawing to the last brush of finish, Célestin Noir composes every cellar as a room of the house — one you will enter, stand in, and know was made for you.

The Atelier

An atelier devoted to the discipline of cellaring.

Célestin Noir is a design and construction atelier working exclusively on private wine environments — cellars, tasting rooms and wine lounges for collectors who treat their collection as a part of the house.

Each commission is led by a single studio, from survey to handover: the same hands that draw the room build it, and the same team returns to care for it.
